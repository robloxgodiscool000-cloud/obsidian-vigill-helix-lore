# StellarGen

> [!summary] Overview
> **StellarGen** is the undisputed corporate hegemony of the post-war era. While officially classified as a transnational megacorporation, it functions on a geopolitical scale as a technocratic global political player. Rather than pursuing malicious dominion, StellarGen operates under a clinical, absolute mandate: the preservation of human life and culture, the containment of environmental hazards that would threaten it, and the total eradication of the [Sanguine Mist](Sanguine%20Mist%201.md).

## Corporate Motives
StellarGen does not view itself as an evil empire, nor does it operate purely for corporate greed. Instead, its upper echelon views the corporation as the *sole competent steward of a dying planet*. Their ideology is built on absolute, unyielding utilitarianism. 
To StellarGen, traditional nation-states failed because they prioritized politics over survival during The Greatest War. Therefore, individual liberties, national borders, and ethical hesitation are seen as luxuries humanity can no longer afford. They believe that a heavily policed, highly structured corporate peace is the only alternative to absolute extinction. They protect and provide to Mutants and humans alike because *every single genome is an asset* to be cataloged, protected, and utilised in the war against the Mist.

## Historical Timeline

### The Foundation (Berlin)
- 2028
Founded in Berlin, Germany, as a biotech firm focusing on advanced genetic therapies, environmental cloning, and synthetic cellular structures. Its early years are defined by ground-breaking medical patents.
### The Defence Contractor Shift
- 2040–2055
As global tensions escalate into the early phases of The Greatest War, StellarGen pivots heavily into military industrialism. They secure contracts with various superpowers to develop advanced technology, ranging from ammunition to targeting systems.
### The Awakening of APEX and NOVA
- 2056
Recognising that the war is destabilizing global economies, the executive board restructures StellarGen into two distinct, specialised pillars: APEX (to generate immense capital and secure resources) and NOVA (to push the boundaries of science).
### The End of the War
- 2057
In a desperate bid to break a global stalemate, dozens of bio-weapons are deployed to render factorial regions uninhabitable, kill crops and more. Millions starve, forcing the Greatest War to a grinding halt, but the chemicals secretly begin to interact with eachother and remnants of battlefields.
### Birth of the Wastelands
- 2058–2059
The dormant bioweapons begin to interact with eachother further and mutate. Puddles and rivers in the worst-hit drop zones thicken into a toxic, rust-coloured sludge. A low-hanging, blood-red fog begins suffocating the post-war ruins, mutating into an aggressive, self-sustaining ecosystem.
### The Threat of Expansion
- 2060
The mist reaches critical mass. The low-hanging fog violently expands into towering, 800-foot-high toxic storm fronts, permanently altering regional weather and birthing the Sanguine Wastelands. Many traditional global governments instantly collapse under the ecological pressure. StellarGen-APEX activates emergency contingency protocols, rapidly constructing the first generation of the Walls protected by their proprietary atmospheric scrubber technology. Alongside APEX, StellarGen-NOVA is shifted towards Sanguine Mist research, though continues its mission of science.
### The Corporate Ascension
- 2061–2080
With some old world nations in complete ruin, many trade their sovereignty to StellarGen in exchange for filtered air, clean water, and physical protection behind the APEX walls. StellarGen seamlessly transitions from a wartime contractor to a global household name in basic life.
### The Current Era
- 2085
StellarGen establishes hovering control over major metropolitan hubs like Berlin. They maintain a tense, cold-war relationship with the Rose Accord and actively hunt down cells of the AWOL Corps, all while funneling trillions into the labs of StellarGen-NOVA to test the limits of known science.

## The Two Branches

### 1. [StellarGen-APEX](The%20Pleromian%20Union%20of%20Labour.md)
APEX is the commercial, industrial, and military face of the corporation. It operates as a ruthless, highly efficient capitalistic machine designed to fund the rest of StellarGen's global infrastructure.
* APEX owns and operates the subsidiary companies that control the safe zones' energy grids, water filtration networks, heavy manufacturing, and atmospheric wall defence systems. 
* APEX Military, whilst acting as a major mercenary branch that is hired by various Remaining Nations to fight in petty disputes, also doubles as the ultimate iron fist of the Corporation. They respond to critical attacks and emergencies on NOVA Sites when the respective Security Department is overwhelmed.

### 2. [StellarGen-NOVA](StellarGen-NOVA%201.md)
NOVA is the primarily non-profit, purely scientific branch of the corporation. If APEX is the muscle and money, NOVA is the mind. Its structure is profoundly similar to a governmental agency. StellarGen-NOVA provides its Research personnel with the circumstances and powers to pursue their own research in order to benefit humanity within the corporate mandated limits.
* NOVA is tasked with the collection, containment, categorisation, and experimentation of all anomalies born from the Mist, including The Blood Eagle Strain and unique mutant biologies. 
* Operating out of Research Sites across the globe, NOVA is exempt from standard commercial pressures. It is all driven by a singular directive: find a permanent cure to rewrite the planet back to its pre-war state.
