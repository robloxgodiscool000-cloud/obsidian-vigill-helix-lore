# District Monad City FERRAPEX

> [!summary] Overview
> **FERRAPHEX** is a colossal, densely populated District Monad City that serves as the central administrative and industrial heartbeat of District 3, formerly known as Boise. Beyond its towering brutalist architecture and sprawling rail networks, it functions as the primary headquarters for the district's branch of the Union of Labour. Because of its sheer scale and strategic importance, FERRAPHEX is a city sharply divided in sentiment; its massive populace holds deeply conflicting opinions regarding its forced **Subsumption** into Pleroma. Operating under constant tension, the city is regularly patrolled by heavily armed detachments of the **Extension Unit**, alongside occasional visits from visiting high-ranking Imperian officials.

## The District Nervous System
FERRAPHEX does not directly serve one singular purpose, much like its sister Monad Cities. Instead, it functions strictly as the central logistical, financial, and bureaucratic nervous system for the entire regional apparatus.

As such, the Pleromian Union of Labour bases its District branch operations from within this city.
