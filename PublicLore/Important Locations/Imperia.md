# Imperia

> [!summary] Overview
> **Imperia** is the ultimate Monad City and the beating heart of Pleroma. As a sprawling, hyper-vertical metropolis that serves as the undisputed capital of the global corporate hegemony. Isolated from reality of the rest of the world, the city has birthed a unique, highly insular civilisation and culture. Its denizens are staggeringly privileged, extraordinarily wealthy, and blissful.

## The Neon Capital
Decades after the Age of Strife, Imperia has been terraformed into an architectural titan of gleaming spires, suspended sky-bridges, and endless cascades of ambient digital displays. Slices of beams of light and cascading holographic art pierce the upper atmosphere, keeping the elite districts in a state of perpetual, sanitised perfection.

As the apex of human civilisation within Pleroma, Imperia enjoys total insulation from the catastrophic state of the dwindling United State of America. While the populace is deeply aware of the governance's absolute power, they view Imperia as the pinnacle of human achievement and the sole sanctuary of culture, fashion, and leisure.

It is also home to the Senate, the central decision-making body of Pleroma.
