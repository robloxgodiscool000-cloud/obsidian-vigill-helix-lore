# "The Lighthouse"

> [!summary] Overview
> The **Re-education Compound - 23**, or **"The Lighthouse"**, is the largest, most well-known, and more importantly, one of the few declassified Re-education Compounds of Pleroma. Due to its nature, and following in example of the other known Re-education Compounds of Pleroma, it is based in District 4. It is formerly a mining quarry of the old State of Alaska, but following the Imperian Subsumption, it has since been renovated into a state-of-the-art facility dedicated to the responsibility of ensuring that even the most rebellious, insubordinate Citizens can be realised into proud, loyal, and shrewd Pleromian patriots.
## Structure
"The Lighthouse" is a large, underground facility built near the southern cliffs of Alaska, acting additionally as a communications and navigational hub for Pleroma's north, hence the name. However, its primary duty is toward the induction and processing of received **"Contaces,"** individuals who have been deemed too rejective towards the Imperian way of life, but not exactly criminal enough to receive **Empyrean Punishment.** The status of a "Contace" is often given to individuals within the Somantic class, or rather, people who are still of use to the nation but require refinement, but this is not exhaustive, and theoretically every citizen of Pleroma may be designated as a Contace. In saying that, however, a highlight of the process within every RC is that the end result is either a continued sentence, or a release as an automatic Somantic, or in certain unsalvageable cases, Empyrean Punishment.

## Average Life
Partly due to its status as the most expansive Re-education Compound in Pleroma, "The Lighthouse" is renowned for its success rate, in terms of the ratio between inductee Contaces and graduate Somantics. Within its confines, all Contaces follow a strict routine, specifically made and authorised by the Pleromian Union of Labour. In coordination with the Pleromian Armed Forces, the entire staff of "The Lighthouse" is made up of exclusively Simulacra Units, ensuring that even the highest echelons within the facility conform to routine-orientations and the perfected, approved interpretation of Imperia's ideals.
