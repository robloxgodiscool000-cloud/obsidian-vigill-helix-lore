# Age of Strife

> [!summary] Overview
> A devastating, spiralled collection of global conflicts that marks the current death spin of old-world nations.

## The Catalyst
Climate collapse and ecosystem failures triggered global shortages of freshwater, arable land, and rare-earth minerals required for advanced technology. Proxy conflicts escalated into direct warfare as major powers competed for resource corridors and strategic production zones.

## Escalation and Desperation
As the conflict dragged on, conventional warfare proved insufficient to break a grinding global stalemate. The traditional superpowers bled each other dry, draining their treasuries, infrastructure, and civilian populations. In a desperate bid to secure a definitive victory, international wartime treaties were abandoned, the United Nations was dissolved and, thus, opening the door for experimental, highly unstable weaponry. 

During this era, Imperia, originally founded in Seattle in 2028, became a premier military contractor and goods company. In quick and murky succession, it rose to become a household megacorporation, and under Mr. Alexandros, became a sovereign nation within the region of the old State of Washington.

## The Aftermath
The Age of Strife ended not with triumph nor with a stoic resolution; it simply ended. The superpowers of the world realised, each in their respective ways and times, that they had done irreparable damage to the globe as a whole. Economies crash and governments progressively lost control after expending to the limits of their armies. Taking advantage of this, roughly in 2031, Imperia successfully coerced the United States to Subsume two more states, having already progressively integrated another.
