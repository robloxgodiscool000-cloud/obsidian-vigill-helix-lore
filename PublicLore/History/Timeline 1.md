## Timeline
Below is a public information, general timeline of Vigill Helix.

### The Pre-War Era
- 2028
Global medical technology leaps forward, spearheaded by biotech firms in hubs like Berlin. Early years of this era are defined by ground-breaking medical patents, advanced technology and hopes for a bright future.
### The Greatest War
- 2040-2055
Global tensions shatter into open conflict. The world's superpowers engage in a devastating, prolonged war over resources and more. Mega-corporations pivot to military industrialism, securing massive contracts to supply advanced weaponry, targeting systems, and combat tech to fuel the fighting.
### The Economic Fracture
- 2056
The sheer scale of the war destabilizes global economies. Foreseeing the collapse of national powers, corporate entities restructure to secure resources, most notably StellarGen's division into APEX and NOVA to maintain capital and technological superiority.
### The Catalyst Drop
- 2057
In a desperate bid to break a global stalemate, dozens of experimental bio-weapons are deployed to render industrial regions uninhabitable and kill crops. Millions starve, forcing the Greatest War to a grinding halt, but the mixed chemicals secretly begin to interact with each other and the remnants of the battlefields.
### Birth of the Wastelands
- 2058-2059
The dormant bioweapons mutate. Puddles and rivers in the worst-hit drop zones thicken into a toxic, rust-coloured sludge. A low-hanging, blood-red fog begins suffocating the post-war ruins, evolving into an aggressive, self-sustaining ecosystem.
### The Global Collapse
- 2060
The mist reaches critical mass, violently expanding into towering, 800-foot-high toxic storm fronts. Regional weather is permanently altered. Traditional global governments collapse under the ecological pressure. In the power vacuum, StellarGen-APEX steps forward, rapidly constructing the first generation of mega-walls protected by proprietary atmospheric scrubbers.
### The Rose Accord Formation
- 2061
Unwilling to surrender their sovereignty to corporate rule, a coalition of Remaining Nations of varying ideologies sign the Rose Accord, putting away old-world feuds to combat a new threat to their ways of life. This fragile alliance is formed to maintain independence and attempt to dismantle StellarGen's monopoly on Mist technology.
### The Current Era
- 2085
A tense, three-way cold war defines the globe. Mega-corporations hold hovering control over major metropolitan hubs like Berlin. They maintain a fragile standoff with the Rose Accord and actively hunt down decentralized cells of the AWOL Corps—all while funneling trillions into labs to test the limits of known science against the raging Sanguine Wastelands outside.
