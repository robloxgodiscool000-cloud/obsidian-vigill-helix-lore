The HIEROPHANT Project is a Roblox roleplay community centred around a player-driven storyline. It is set within a dystopian world where a corporatist government has taken hold of five territories to safeguard and rule. As the old nations of the continent falter following a global catastrophe, Pleroma stands as a beacon of a new, unfettered future.
