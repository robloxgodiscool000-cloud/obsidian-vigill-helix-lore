An **Original Character (OC)** is a unique, custom persona created by a player to participate in the ongoing story of The HIEROPHANT Project. It is unlike a standard character within THP because it is completely up to the writer of the character on how they want their character to behave, the circumstances of their background, and their fate.
To maintain the integrity, consistency, and realism of the setting, there are a few regulations you must follow before interacting with the lore in-character.

### Starting Roles
- Depending on personal choice, your OC will begin as either a **New Folk** or a standard **Native-Born**.
    - **New Folk** are individuals who have arrived to FERRAPHEX _after_ the Imperian Subsumption.
    - **Native-Born** are individuals who were residing in FERRAPHEX _before_ the Imperian Subsumption.
- From there, your OC can join the _Pleromian Union of Labour_ to become a **Union Member,** or also known as a **Worker.**
### Origins & Species
1. Your OC must originate from HIEROPHANT's Earth. Characters from other planets, alternate dimensions, or different realities are strictly prohibited.

2. Your OC must **fall** into one of the following categories:
   ***NB: This means they will be classified as one of the four in lore and in interactions with Actor Characters, but it is not exhaustive nor absolutely precise.***
    
    - **Pleromian Citizen:** The legal & traditional Imperian designation for all organic, natural-born citizens within Pleroma. The baseline.
        
    - **Somantics:** The legal designation for Imperian-vetted Pleromian Citizens, thus, by extension, residents of a Monad City. Somantics are utterly bound to their designated Monad City, and are not permitted to leave unless given specific permission. These are citizens that have been selected to be suitable Workers, though it does not guarantee entrance into the Union of Labour. FERRAPHEX Somantics uniquely feature differential individuals consisting not just of humans but also of Aberrants, Simulacra Units, compared to other Monad Cities. 
        
    - **Simulacra Units:** The legal Imperian designation for synthetic, manufactured beings. Simulacra are colloquially known as _Lacs_ or _Sims_, and individual Simulacra are defined as a Simulacrum Unit. Simulacrum are considered the lowest within Pleromian society, as they have been made for specific ends and the extents of their sentiency is up to debate. Within FERRAPHEX, Simulacra Units face hardships due to discrimination and prejudice.
        
    - **Aberrants:** The colloquial term for mutated, hybridised, or otherwise non-baseline organic individuals within Pleroma's borders. Aberrants come in many shapes and forms, but because Imperia institutes policies to ensure they are recognised as Pleromian Citizens, however, this does not free them of societal and personal contempt.

### Factions & Behaviour
- Your OC must demonstrate a realistic value for their own life to begin with.

- Your OC cannot be designed as an active, unprovoked, or immediate threat to other characters.
