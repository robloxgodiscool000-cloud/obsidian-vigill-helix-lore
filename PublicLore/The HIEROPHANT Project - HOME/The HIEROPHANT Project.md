# World Overview

> [!summary] Core Concept
> The HIEROPHANT Project is a roleplay community centred around a player-driven storyline. It is set within a dystopian world where a corporatist government has taken hold of five territories to safeguard and rule. As the old nations of the continent falter following a global catastrophe, Pleroma stands as a beacon of a new, unfettered future.

## Historical Synopsis
The world of The HIEROPHANT Project is set within a version of Earth. Following catastrophic pandemics, resources wars, famines, and various disasters, the superpowers of the world have been utterly crippled. The global economy has collapsed, leading to dozens of nations falling into pandemonium & anarchy. The continental landform of North America, in particular, is experiencing a rampant, expanding combination of soil degradation, atmospheric chaos, and epidemical phenomena across the nations within it.

## The Dying Nation & The Rising Phoenix
As of 2026, following the catastrophes facing the globe, the faltering nation of The United States of America is waning in power. Despite once being a paramount superpower in the world, it now faces domestic cataclysms. The economy nosedives, and famine spreads across its states. But the borders of the US, and the accompanying issues, end at Imperia. Following an unheard-of federal campaign between 2021 to 2024 spearheaded by the up-and-coming Mr. Alexandros, due to the desperate status of the US, the megacorporation known as Imperia successfully, legitimately bought the entire state of Washington. The former state took the name of its new governance: **Imperia.**

## Current Day: 2038
Today, in 2038, Imperia has become a shining beacon of civilisation and order amidst the anarchistic wreckage of the old world nation of the United States. Taking advantage of the United State's continued worsening circumstances, Imperia has recently acquired the former US states of Idaho (District 3), and Alaska (District 4), incorporating them into the newly coined nation of **Pleroma.** These new Districts, and their populations, must grapple with their new identities alongside ensuring their newly implicated output quotas are met.
