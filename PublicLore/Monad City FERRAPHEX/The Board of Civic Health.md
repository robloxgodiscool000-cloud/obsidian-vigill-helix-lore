# Board of Civic Health (BoCH)

> [!summary] Overview
> **The Board of Civic Health or BoCH** is the absolute medical authority and biological caretaker of the Monad City. Functioning as a massive, unified Sub-Union that monopolises both pharmaceutical manufacturing and all hospital staff, the Board ensures the workforce remains functional and the population free of contagion. Operating with cold, utilitarian efficiency, their mandate is to maintain the physical viability of all regional assets while contributing to Pleromian science.

## Sub-Union Structure
To manage the staggering health requirements of a hyper-dense urban population, the Board is partitioned into several specialised divisions. While their operations span numerous logistical and chemical departments, their two primary branches consist of:
### 1. Medical Personnel
This branch forms the hands-on, clinical backbone of the Board. Operating with a singular, pragmatic goal, they provide comprehensive medical, psychological, and surgical care across the city's hospital complexes, tenement clinics, and industrial triage centres.

The standard of care is strictly utilitarian, designed to stabilise address as many unique biologies as possible, including the biomechanical and mechanical makeups of Simulacra.

### 2. Theoretical & Primary Science Unit (TPSU)
Serving as the academic and experimental core of the city, and entirely based out of it, this unit is dedicated to advanced pharmaceutical development and biological research. Insulated from the daily grind of the triage clinics, these academics and scientists focus heavily on analysing mutations, developing new atmospheric countermeasures, and synthesising the mandatory chemical supplements distributed to the populace.

This unit, due to its highly specialised nature, is not found outside of FERRAPHEX, but it is known for the Unit's personnel to venture out on expeditions.
