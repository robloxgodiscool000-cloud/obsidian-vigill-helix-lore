# Magistrate's Hall (MH)

> [!summary] Overview
> **The Magistrate's Hall** sits at the absolute apex of the Monad City's hierarchy, and the hierarchy of the District 3 Pleromian Union of Labour. Functioning as the paramount governance entity for the entire city, it is the smallest, most restrictive, and most elite Sub-Union within the Union of Labour. It serves as the vital federal bridge connecting the dictates of Imperia to the daily industrial grind of FERRAPEX and the wider expanse of District 3. The command structure is bifurcated into two absolute authorities: the **Magistrate**, who maintains an iron grip over the city itself, and the **Governor**, who is based within the Hall but dictates the operational quotas and logistics for the entirety of the District.

## The Halls of Governance
To maintain seamless bureaucratic control and project Imperia's will over the masses, the Hall is divided into highly specialised, exclusive branches:

### 1. The Magistrate's Office
Acting as the administrative and diplomatic engine of the Hall, this branch is composed of the elite personal attendants, secretaries, and high-level aides serving the Magistrate and the Governor. While the dual leaders make grand strategic decisions, this office executes them with ruthless efficiency. They conduct rigorous financial and operational audits on all other Sub-Unions, manage highly classified federal matters, and serve as the pristine, official escorts whenever high-ranking Imperia officials descend upon the smog-choked city.

### 2. The Judicial Branch
Working in lethal conjunction with the Urban Prefecture, the Judicial Branch is the absolute legal authority within the Monad City. They enact, modify, and fabricate law at will to suit the immediate needs of the Hall and the overarching demands of Imperia. They manage the city's legal framework, preside over trials, and orchestrate the highly publicised public executions that are deemed necessary to maintain absolute terror and compliance among the working-class populace.
