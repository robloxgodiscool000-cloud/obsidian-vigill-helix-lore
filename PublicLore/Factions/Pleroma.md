# The Sovereign Nation of Pleroma

> [!summary] Overview
>The Sovereign Nation of Pleroma is a new-age nation born out of the dying giant of the United States of America. Originally an enclave known as Imperia, which is now its capital city, it has exponentially expanded outwards, from the Subsumption the former State of Washington to recently Subsuming the former States of Montana and Alaska. As a nation itself, however, Pleroma roughly possesses 9 million registered citizens. The former states Pleroma now rules are divided into Districts (1-4), with the borders of each being defined by high concrete walls and extended railway lines.

## Governance
* **Pleroma** is ruled, centrally, by the Senate, which holds authority over all Pleromian Districts. The Senatorial Building is built in Imperia, and its members include roughly 300 Senators & adjacent leaders, though the Four Governors of the Districts hold the power to initialise motions, veto them and hold the strongest voting powers.
* Membership of the Senate is highly restrictive, and the majority of the relevant members are sourced from the Higher Districts (1 & 2). All Governors and Magistrates are automatically Senators.
* The Senate is utilised as a discussion body, in accordance with the Four Governors, who hold  the power to debate and voice their concerns over proposed governmental motions, and laws.
* The Council of Four, originally made up of the closest confidants of the late Mr. Alexandros, are elected by the Senate, and serve for a life tenure. Currently, though not a required merit, all four Councillors serve as Governors.
* The Magistrates of the Monad Cities are another vital governmental body positioned directly under the Four Governor, though unofficial. As Magistrates, they rule the respective Monad Cities of the Districts, and hold immense fiscal power, as Monad Cities serve as critical junctures for District commerce, alongside serving as the highest population centres of their regions.
## Technology, Culture & Arts
Pleroma is a unique nation of differing, competing cultures. Due to the fact it was the first Pleromian city, and as the heart of wider Pleroma, Imperia has developed an intense, almost alien culture in comparison to its Districts. Relatively, the earlier the Subsumption into Pleroma, the more alike the District is to Imperia, such as District 1 which has largely become synonymous with Imperia.
### Imperia
Marked by the fact it is the oldest Pleromian city, it is the baseline to which Pleroma's arts, cultures, and technology has developed. It styles itself as a hyper-modern, progressive city, though through its architecture and the personal aspirations of its people, it incorporates many aspects of the public's fantasticial, romantic rendition of Rome. High-rising, pristine towers of white combined with pillars, alongside statues styled alike to Greco-Roman instances within its parks. Centrally, though, and especially unique to Imperia, the populace tends to praise & remember Mr. Alexandros as a sort of mythical, divine figure. This perception sometimes gives way to a quasi-religion based around old Americana-Christianity, Greco-Roman mysticism, and humanist centrality. Additionally, in Senate proceedings and especially high class interactions, an entirely new language is utilised.

### District 1 - 2
As the Higher Districts, One and Two are quite similar to Imperia in terms of technology. Their Monad Cities are clearly in the later stages of Imperia-ification, defined by a mix of standard architecture with looming, pristine skyscrapers. In rural areas, however, and excluding Monad Cities, One and Two are characteristically "normal." Excluding exceptions, the majority of 1 - 2 still resemble the old States they hold their origin to.

### District 3 - 4
As the Lower Districts, with District 3 being the only truly habitable one, both are marked with a friction between Imperian technology, culture, behaviour and arts. Due to the fact they are still undergoing **Subsumption**, they still hold a distinct lifestyle, far more reminiscent of old-world America.

## Military
The Pleromian Armed Forces fashions itself not dissimilar to its heritage as a nation born out of the US, but it takes & utilises the heavy, corporate and pragmaticism of its megacorporation heritage in regards to its exact structure. An example would be the fact it splits many of its assets and personnel into Detachments, or alternatively, if the situation requires it, an Extension Unit. A key, notable aspect would be the fact the entire Armed Forces uses a marked higher amount of Simulacra Units within its ranks, unlike its sister organisation, the Union of Labour.
