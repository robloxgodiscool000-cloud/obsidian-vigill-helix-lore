# The Pleromian Union of Labour

> [!summary] Overview
> **The Union of Labour** is the colossal, omnipresent administrative and industrial apparatus that manages the working-class population of Pleroma. Operating with absolute, suffocating authority across the Lower Districts, the Union functions as functionally and culturally distinct entity from the pristine upper echelons of Imperia. Though it manages the daily survival, housing, and labour administration of millions, it exists solely as an extension of Imperia's vast machine.
