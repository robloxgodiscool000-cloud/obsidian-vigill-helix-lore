# The Extension Unit

> [!summary] Overview
> **The Extension Unit** is a military detachment of Imperia's Armed Forces, deployed specifically to District 3 to act as the Senate's iron fist and ensure absolute compliance across the territory. Based outside the limits of FERRAPHEX, their massive garrison lines the colossal perimeter walls that outline the district it resides in.
## Organisational Structure
Operating under a rigid corporate-paramilitary hybrid structure, the Extension Unit treats tactical force as a quantifiable asset designed to maximise territorial leverage and guarantee systemic order. Led by a Commandant of Regional Security, the Unit functions as a bridge to alleviate the gap between Imperia and the Lower District of Three, answering directly to the upper echelons of Imperia.
## Technological Supremacy 
The armaments of the Extension Unit is instantly recognisable: sleek, minimalist, and terrifyingly efficient. Standing in stark contrast to the standard gear of the Urban Prefecture and adjacent Union of Labour forces, they utilise proprietary, high-end technology built to withstand numerous border frontiers and urban warfare alike.
Because their human personnel numbers are heavily resourced and strictly maintained, the Unit heavily supplements its forces with Simulacra.

## Operational Divisions
To efficiently police the district boundaries and secure the borderlands, the Extension Unit is divided into multiple branches. The two most notable branches are below:

 1. ***Wall Infantry***
The largest and most visible branch, tasked with the gruelling, constant defence of the massive perimeter walls that protect District 3. They manage the border and checkpoint security, serving as the first line of defence against hostile incursions alongside ensuring all refuges entering into Pleroma are safely processed.

 2. ***Tactical Response Brigade (TRB)***
Elite, heavily armed strike teams stationed at strategic gate-houses along the wall. They are deployed directly into the toxic exterior when high-value corporate targets or internal security breaches demand immediate intervention, acting as a rapid-deployment hammer against any threat to Pleroma's national security.
