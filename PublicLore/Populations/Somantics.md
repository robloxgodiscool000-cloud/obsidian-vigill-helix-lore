# Somantics

> [!summary] Overview
> **Somantics**, frequently referred to colloquially as **Prospects**, are individuals who have officially elevated their status from base-level Citizens of Pleroma to legally bound residents of Monad Cities. While the general populace consists of transient visitors, **New Folk**, and **Native-Born** citizens, only those who officially register as Somantics are integrated into FERRAPHEX's infrastructure. By surrendering their freedom of movement, Somantics are granted state-designated housing and become the exclusive pool from which Imperia sources its **Union Workers**.

## Standard Pipeline
To maintain the relentless pace of industry expected of a Monad City, FERRAPHEX requires a continuous influx of fresh labour and commerce. Consequently, base-level citizens are heavily incentivised to transition into Somantics. They are routinely sourced from the rural territories of District 3, though, as it also serves as a border sector, many are also processed directly from the refugee populations arriving from outside Pleroma.

These individuals legally forfeit their right to leave the city, amongst other privileges, placing their lives entirely under the jurisdiction of Imperia in exchange for finer quality of life and the prospect of upward mobility. 

## Consistency
Somantics of FERRAPHEX, and to a lesser extent District 3, are unique as they possess a higher amount of various groups evident in Pleroma, such as vetted Simulacra and Aberrants.
