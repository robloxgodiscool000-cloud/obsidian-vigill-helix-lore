# Simulacra Units

> [!summary] Overview
> **Simulacra Units**, frequently referred to colloquially as **Sims** or **Lacs**, are synthetic, manufactured beings. Individual Simulacra are defined as a Simulacrum Unit. Simulacra Units are considered the lowest within Pleromian society, as they have been made for specific ends and the extents of their sentiency is up to debate. Within FERRAPHEX, Simulacra Units face hardships due to discrimination and prejudice.

## Standard Pipeline
Most Simulacra Units are sourced directly from Imperia, and neighbouring District factories. Simulacra Units are not, however, a proprietary technology of Pleroma; rather, during and before the Age of Strife, android-like humanoids were consistently used in an effort to relieve regular humans of highly dangerous and tedious jobs.

Imperia welcomes any Simulacra Units wishing to become a Somantic Citizen, however, it still produces their own Simulacra. As such, there is a constant, noticeable friction between the Simulacrum population and human population, based out of the fact Imperia still utilises Simulacra as disposable labour androids.

Regardless, many Simulacra Units are evident within FERRAPHEX, and beyond.
## Common Appearances & Characteristics
Simulacra Units are known for uniform appearances and traits, due to the nature of their creation. Most Unit Lines, as part of their creation process, use brain scans of selected Somantic Citizens to act as a base for which Imperia, corporations and nations may mold to fit the specific task assigned to the Unit Line.

For example, a common Simulacra Unit Line is the **PAVO** Unit Line. PAVOs are an Imperian-sponsored VIP protection unit, commonly seen guarding select higher echelon members of the Union of Labour. They are known for their aristocratic-like demeanour, acting as dutiful attendants and guardsmen for the whims of their assigned personnel.
