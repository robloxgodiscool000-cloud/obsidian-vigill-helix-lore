# District

> [!summary] Overview
> An exception to the other Districts' quality of life, **the average civilian life** within District 1 is remarkably comfortable and highly advanced. As the smallest District and the District that houses Imperia, alongside the fact it was the first to be Subsumed, the average citizen enjoys a vibrant, high-tech existence.

## Existence
As the District containing the heart of Pleroma, Imperia has produced a unique culture that which has spread to the rest of the former state. Within Imperia, life resembles a highly functional, sanitised cyber-metropolis. Within District 1 in general, life is comfortable, though Imperia's presence leads to a highly controlled environment to ensure utter loyalty and security.
## Culture & Average Life
Because a large portion of the populace is insulated from the horrors of the dying US and uncertainty that leads to physical altercation evident within the Lower Districts, the insular culture is entirely consumed by petty merchant politics, hedonistic cultural events, and entertainment.

# District 2

> [!summary] Overview
> A step above to the Lower Districts' quality of life, **the average civilian life** within District 2 is quite comfortable and unregulated, not much unlike its former existence as the State of Oregon. As the second largest District, Two features quite an abundance of agriculture operations and energy operations. As the second District to be Subsumed, the average citizen enjoys a hard-working, but fair, lifestyle, alongside feeding off the special attention from Imperia.

# District 3

> [!summary] Overview
> The first of the Lower Districts, and one of the two to be most recently Subsumed into Pleroma, **the average civilian life** within District 3 is somewhat stifling. As Pleroma aims to expand further, this District faces the monumental task of being the primary breadbasket of the nation. It is heavily policed and monitored by Imperia, especially due to the Monad City of FERRAPHEX. As a District undergoing Subsumption, the opinions within is expectedly divided between its citizens, with them grappling with their new identities as Pleromian citizens in their own ways. It is the former State of Montana.
# District 4

> [!summary] Overview
> The second of the Lower Districts, and one of the two to be next Subsumed, **the average, normal civilian life,** as seen in other Districts, within District 4 is nearly non-existent. Due to the aftermath of the Age of Strife, the former State of Alaska's weather and geography was changed radically. District 4 inherits this, and it is known for its frigidly cold winds that bite at unprotected skin with ice shards and blizzards that immediately cover any land bases. As such, Imperia has designated District 4 as a restricted, subterranean sector, utilising it for means unfitting for the rest of its Districts. Military, research, and  colonies are a common sight on the southern side of the District.
