# Aberrants

> [!summary] Overview
> **Aberrants** is the colloquial term for mutated, hybridised, or otherwise non-baseline organic individuals within Pleroma's borders. They are the surviving descendants and victims of the various bioweapons, plagues, and unique phenomena that were deployed or occurred during the Age of Strife. They are largely legally vulnerable, and face severe societal discrimination within dense Monad Cities like FERRAPHEX.

## Origins
The Aberrant population was born from the aftermath of the Age of Strife. When the globe underwent catastrophic resource wars and natural disasters, numerous bioweapons were deployed. Millions died, however, a percentage of the population survived prolonged exposure, their DNA violently rewritten by the bioweapons. This mutation process was not uniform; the various instances of bioweaponry reacted unpredictably to individual genetics, resulting in a vast and chaotic spectrum of physical and biological alterations that have been passed down through generations.
