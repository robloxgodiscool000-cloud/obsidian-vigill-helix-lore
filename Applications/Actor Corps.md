The Actor Corps play the Project's **Actor Characters**: the officials, officers and figures who bring Pleroma's institutions to life in roleplay. Actors often take on several characters, switch between them at short notice, and play roles they may not personally like, so that other players have a world to react to.

## About you
- What timezone are you in, and when are you usually available to play? {short}
- Describe your roleplay experience, especially any time you played characters for other people's benefit, such as running a scene, playing staff characters or game-mastering. {long 300}

## Playing many roles
- How comfortable are you playing several different characters, sometimes within the same session? {choice: Very comfortable | Comfortable with some practice | Not very comfortable yet}

- How do you feel about playing characters whose views you find disagreeable, such as an oppressive Prefecture officer? How do you keep that in character and keep it fun for everyone? {long 400}
- An Actor Character's job is often to make other players' stories better rather than to win. Describe a time you set up someone else's moment, or how you would. {long 300}
